[Dossier: 10 BCM Cost-Efficiency | Overview: Target & Strategy]
Achieving 10 Billion Cubic Meters (BCM) in annual water savings across the Amu Darya basin at the lowest possible cost requires evaluating standalone infrastructure and agricultural pathways against a combined hybrid strategy.
---
[Dossier: 10 BCM Cost-Efficiency | Option A: Primary Canal Lining]
Lining the upper 20% to 30% of high-seepage primary canals saves roughly 4 to 7 BCM annually. Installed geomembrane lining (~$5 to $12/m2) is far cheaper than concrete ($15 to $25/m2), yet relying on canal lining alone across thousands of kilometers of main trunks requires a multi-billion-dollar investment to reach 10 BCM.
---
[Dossier: 10 BCM Cost-Efficiency | Option B: Cotton Drip Conversion]
Converting furrow-irrigated cotton to subsurface drip saves ~6,000 m3 per hectare. Reaching 10 BCM purely through drip irrigation requires converting roughly 1.6 million hectares; at $1,200 to $2,500 per hectare, total capital costs range from $1.9 Billion to $4.0 Billion USD.
---
[Dossier: 10 BCM Cost-Efficiency | Option C Step 1: Leveling & Crops]
In the optimal 10 BCM hybrid pathway, Step 1 pairs low-cost operational shifts with laser land leveling (~$50/ha across hundreds of thousands of hectares) and converting 200,000 hectares of paddy rice to wheat, saving roughly 2 to 3 BCM at minimal structural cost.
---
[Dossier: 10 BCM Cost-Efficiency | Option C Step 2: Strategic Lining]
In Step 2 of the optimal 10 BCM hybrid pathway, lining the highest-loss sandy stretches of primary arterial canals (such as upper segments of the Karakum Canal and regional feeder trunks) with geomembranes captures 4 to 5 BCM of annual water savings.
---
[Dossier: 10 BCM Cost-Efficiency | Option C Step 3: Targeted Drip & Total Cost]
Step 3 subsidizes drip irrigation for high-yield commercial cotton farms to capture the final 2 to 3 BCM. Total hybrid cost is ~$1.5B to $2.8B USD via state budgets and development banks (World Bank / ADB), making targeted geomembrane lining plus rice-to-wheat switching the cheapest options per cubic meter saved.
