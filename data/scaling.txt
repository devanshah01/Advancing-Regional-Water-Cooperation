[Dossier: Amu Darya Scaling Rules | Rule 1: Canal Lining Scaling]
Total annual transit seepage across unlined Amu Darya canals is ~20 to 25 BCM out of ~70-80 BCM flow. Lining 1% of primary/secondary canals saves ~0.25 to 0.35 BCM (250-350 MCM)/year. Required lining % = Target BCM / 0.30 (e.g., 10 BCM requires ~30-33% of main canals lined).
---
[Dossier: Amu Darya Scaling Rules | Rule 2: Cotton Drip Scaling]
Flood-irrigated cotton uses ~12,000 m3/ha vs. ~5,000 m3/ha under subsurface drip, saving 7,000 m3 (0.007 BCM) per converted hectare. Hectares needed = (Target BCM * 1,000,000,000) / 7,000 (e.g., 3 BCM requires ~428,570 hectares converted to drip).
---
[Dossier: Amu Darya Scaling Rules | Rule 3: Rice-to-Wheat Scaling]
Paddy rice uses ~18,000 m3/ha vs. ~5,000 m3/ha for winter wheat, saving 13,000 m3 (0.013 BCM) per converted hectare. Hectares to switch = (Target BCM * 1,000,000,000) / 13,000 (e.g., 2 BCM requires switching ~153,840 hectares from rice to wheat).
---
[Dossier: Amu Darya Scaling Rules | Rule 4: Lining & Drip Costs]
Average geomembrane canal lining costs ~$8.50/m2 (~$850,000/km for a 10m-wide canal covering 100,000 m2/km). Drip irrigation capital averages ~$1,800/hectare, so Total Drip Cost = Required Hectares * $1,800.
